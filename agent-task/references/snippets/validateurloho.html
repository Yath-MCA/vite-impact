Welcome to IMPACT, the online proofing tool that permits authors to directly edit and collaborate on book content for Oxford University Press.

Instructions

  1. Proof for Review: To access the proof click “Agree and Continue” below.
  2. Respond to Queries: Ensure all copyeditor’s queries are addressed via “Queries” (left panel), or AQ symbols in the Editor View (centre panel).
  3. Edit Proof: Make all required corrections in the Editor View (centre panel).
  4. Proof for Layout: Review the typeset proof layout (right panel) and instruct any layout-related corrections as comments in the Editor View (centre panel).
  5. Finalize: To approve the proof and proceed publication select “Finalize” (top-right).

Notes

  • The images displayed in the Editor View are low resolution to optimise the performance of the tool. Please view the typeset proof layout to view images in high resolution.
  • The comment functionality allows you to provide instruction to the production editor or typesetting team to include the ability to supply files as attachments.
  • Post finalisation you will:
    • Receive a copy of the proof with your changes tracked via email for your own records.
    • Be requested to complete a short survey on the proofing tool, the results of which will be used to inform future improvements.
  • For the smooth operation of our system, we recommend an internet connection speed of at least 10mbps.

    If you need assistance with IMPACT, please review the Guided Tour, FAQs, User Guide, and Video Tutorial here, or contact our support team at impact.helpdesk@newgen.co
