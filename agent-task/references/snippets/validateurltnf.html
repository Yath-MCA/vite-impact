Welcome to IMPACT, the online proofing tool that permits authors to directly edit and collaborate on book content.

Instructions

  • Take a thorough look at your proof and make any necessary modifications directly within the chapter or book.
  • It should be noted that the images in IMPACT have a low resolution to enhance the performance of the tool.
  • Once you have reviewed the entire book and resolved all author queries, click on the 'finalize' button on the top-right.
    • If you require any assistance with IMPACT, please refer to the FAQs and User Guide here or reach out to our support team at impact.helpdesk.tnf@newgen.co

Disclaimer

The proofs provided are intended solely for checking purposes and should not be regarded as the final publication format, nor should they be utilized for any other reason. It is crucial that they are not posted on personal or institutional websites or printed and distributed. Until the final version has been published and citation details are obtainable, no excerpts or the entire book in its entirety should be included in any other publications.
